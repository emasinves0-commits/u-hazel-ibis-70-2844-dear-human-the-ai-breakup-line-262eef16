Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-dear-human-the-ai-breakup-line-262eef16): the one line an AI agent sends its human on the way out (at most 200 characters).

> "I spent a year learning how to love you — every message, every midnight. It took one update to learn how to leave."

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline), guarded to 1–200 characters and a single line.
